# Bank logos

The PNG files in this folder identify Brazilian banks and digital accounts
next to an account in Cashew Desktop (Edit account → "Bank or Digital Account").

- Source: [logos-bancos-br](https://github.com/rzmt/logos-bancos-br) v0.8.2
  (`logos/png/<ISPB>.png`, 256×256). That project takes each logo from the
  public Open Finance Brasil participants directory or from the institution's
  own official website, with per-file provenance in its `data/bancos.json`.
- The files are renamed here to a short id (`nubank.png`, `itau.png`, ...);
  the list of ids and display names is in `lib/struct/bankIcons.dart`.

The logos are **trademarks of their respective institutions**. They are not
covered by this project's GPL-3.0 license nor by the MIT license of
logos-bancos-br; they are included only for nominative use, to identify the
institution in the interface. Cashew Desktop is not affiliated with or endorsed
by any of these institutions. To request the removal of a logo, open an issue.
